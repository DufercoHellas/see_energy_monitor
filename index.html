📐 Plant Running Cost Formulas (SRMC)

1. Gas Plant Running Cost Formula:
Running Cost (€/MWh) = (Gas_Price / η) + (EUA × 0.202 / η) + VOM
• 0.202 tCO2/MWh_th: Natural gas stoichiometric emission factor.
• η: Plant net electrical efficiency (e.g. 63.5% Komotini Power, 63.0% Ag. Nikolaos II, 58.2% Megalopolis V, 52.5% Lavrio IV).
• VOM: Variable Operation & Maintenance (€2.50/MWh).
2. Lignite Plant Running Cost Formula:
Running Cost (€/MWh) = (Lignite_Cost / η) + (EUA × 0.400 / η) + VOM
• 0.400 tCO2/MWh_th: Greek lignite carbon intensity (~0.96 t/MWh_e for Ptolemaida V).
• Lignite Fuel Cost: €18.00/MWh_th (PPC audited mine-mouth cost).
• η: 41.5% (Ptolemaida V Ultra-supercritical), 34.0% (Ag. Dimitrios / Megalopolis IV).
• VOM: €4.50/MWh.
